Who we are

One lab, two groups

Founded in 2000 and led by Prof. Dr. Oliver Kohlbacher. Both groups work side by side in the Tübingen AI Research Building.

abi

Applied Bioinformatics

University of Tübingen

Analysis of omics data (genomics, proteomics, metabolomics), structural bioinformatics and computational immunomics, with a reputation for high-quality research software.

Meet the ABI team →
tbi

Translational Bioinformatics

University Hospital Tübingen

At the intersection of medical informatics and bioinformatics. As part of DIFUTURE, the group builds Data Integration Centers for the German Medical Informatics Initiative.

What we work on

Research areas

All research →

Computational Immunomics

MHC ligandomics, HLA typing and neoepitope discovery from mass spectrometry and NGS data.

Computational Mass Spectrometry

Algorithms and the OpenMS platform for high-throughput proteomics and metabolomics.

Personalized Medicine

Data integration for molecular tumor boards across 26 university hospitals (DNPM).

Structural Bioinformatics

Computer-aided drug design, cheminformatics and prediction of protein-protein complexes.

Translational Bioinformatics

Bridging molecular and clinical data, e.g. federated, privacy-preserving analytics with FLAME.
