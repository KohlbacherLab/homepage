How To Find Us

By Car
  • Follow the B27 toward Tübingen from Stuttgart
  • Take the "Tübingen, Nordstadt, Kliniken" exit
  • At the 3rd traffic light, turn left onto Wilhelmstraße
  • Turn right onto "Nordring" (uphill)
  • At the 4th traffic light, turn left onto Waldhäuser Straße / Obere Viehweide
  • Turn left at the first opportunity into Friedrich-Miescher-Straße
  • Turn right into Maria-von-Linden-Straße — the building is approx. 30m ahead on the right
  • Parking is available behind the building via a ramp on the left
By Bus
  • From Tübingen Hauptbahnhof, cross the street to the "Europaplatz" bus stops
  • Take Bus Line 3 toward "Waldhäuser Ost" (approx. 15 minutes)
  • Exit at "Sternwarte"
  • Follow Paul-Ehrlich-Straße and turn left onto Maria-von-Linden-Straße
  • The building is at number 6 on the right side
By Train
  • Arrive at Tübingen Hauptbahnhof
  • The main bus station is directly across from the station exit
  • Take Bus Line 3 (see above)
By Airplane
  • Arrive at Stuttgart Airport
  • Take Airport-Sprinter Bus Line 828 to Tübingen Hauptbahnhof (approx. 60 minutes)
  • Then follow the bus directions above
