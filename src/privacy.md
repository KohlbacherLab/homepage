## 2. Hosting and Server Logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco,
CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser transmits technical data that GitHub records in server logs:

- IP address
- date and time of the request
- requested page or file
- referrer URL
- browser type and version, operating system

This data is required to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is
Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the reliable and secure operation of the website. GitHub may
process the data in the USA. GitHub Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so an adequate
level of data protection exists (Art. 45 GDPR). For details see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not set cookies and does not use tracking or analytics tools. Fonts, icons and the site
search are delivered from this website itself; no content from third parties is embedded.

Your choice between light and dark mode is stored in your browser's local storage (key
`vitepress-theme-appearance`). It stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. This storage
is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

## 4. Links to External Websites

This website links to external services, for example GitHub, publishers and partner institutions. Data is only
transmitted to these providers when you follow such a link. Their own privacy policies apply from then on.

## 5. Contact by Email or Phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details (e.g. name, email address, the content of your
message) to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or
pre-contractual measures, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR otherwise; our legitimate interest is answering your request.
We delete the data once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

## 6. Your Rights

- obtain access to your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR)
- have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16 GDPR)
- have your data erased (Art. 17 GDPR)
- restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, contact the controller named above.
